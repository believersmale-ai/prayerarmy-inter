How check-in works

  1. A registrar approves your member account and links it to your church record.
  2. Choose your service when its check-in window opens.
  3. Enter the current eight-character code displayed at the service.
  4. Look for your confirmation. If you need help, an usher can check you in.

Each member checks in to their own record. Your household and other members' details stay private.
