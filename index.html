OUR FOUNDATION

Built on prayer, truth and faith.

Who we are, where we are headed, and the convictions that hold us together.

OUR MISSION

To raise people of prayer, faith and spiritual purpose.

Prayer Army International exists to proclaim Jesus Christ, cultivate a life of prayer, teach God's Word, develop believers for purposeful Christian living, and equip people to serve their generation with faith, courage and love.

OUR VISION

To raise generals who transform generations.

We envision a growing community of Spirit-filled believers who know God, walk in truth, pray with confidence, live with integrity and become a positive spiritual influence in their families, communities and nations.

OUR VALUES

Prayer. Word. Faith. Love. Service.

We value a life of prayer, biblical truth, faith in God, Christlike love, integrity, excellence, discipleship, evangelism and practical service to people.

STATEMENT OF FAITH

What we believe

We believe in one God, eternally existing as Father, Son and Holy Spirit, the Creator of heaven and earth and the source of all life.

We believe in Jesus Christ, the Son of God, who came in the flesh, died for our sins, rose again, and is Lord and Saviour of all who believe in Him.

We believe in the Holy Spirit, who convicts, regenerates, indwells, guides and empowers believers to live holy lives and serve God's purposes.

We believe the Bible is God's Word, trustworthy and authoritative for faith, doctrine, character and Christian living.

We believe in salvation by grace through faith in Jesus Christ, repentance from sin, the new birth and a life that reflects the character of Christ.

We believe in the Great Commission: that every believer is called to share the gospel, make disciples and demonstrate God's love through service.
