Good to know

Questions before you begin?

Learn how the Academy works and what to expect when joining.

Who can access the Academy?

Learning access requires an approved student or member account. Published courses and enrollment eligibility are controlled by Academy administrators.

Can I explore courses before signing in?

Yes. When the public catalog is enabled, visitors can browse published course descriptions and preview lesson headings. Signing in is required to enroll and study.

Are examinations and certificates available?

Academy administrators control when assessments open and when certification features are enabled. Availability may vary by course.

How do I continue learning after a break?

Select My Learning, sign in with your approved account and return to your enrolled courses and progress dashboard.
