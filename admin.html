🌐 Social Media Channels

Input and update your ministry's social media profile URLs. These automatically render as interactive brand icons in the website footer and contact sections.

Visitor tracking and retargeting (Meta Pixel)

Paste your Meta Pixel ID to count visits and to build "people who visited our website" audiences in Meta Ads Manager (Facebook and Instagram). Visitors are asked for permission first, and nothing loads for people who decline. Only names of actions are sent to Meta (such as "viewed a sermon"), never names or messages. Only a System admin can change this.

In Meta Events Manager, open Data sources, choose your Pixel, and copy the ID (digits only). Leave empty to turn tracking off.
