Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Prayer Army International respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information you share through this website.

Information we collect

When you contact us, submit a prayer request, share a testimony, register interest in an event, or use another form, we may collect the details you provide, such as your name, email address, phone number, message, and prayer or testimony content.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your questions, prayer requests, and contact messages.
  • To manage event registrations and ministry communications you request.
  • To review and publish testimonies only when you give permission for publication.
  • To protect the website, prevent misuse, and improve our services.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with trusted service providers who help us operate the website or deliver a service you request, and when disclosure is required by law.

Forms, cookies, and external services

Information submitted through this website may be processed by the hosting, email, database, and other services used to operate the site. Those services may use essential cookies or similar technologies to keep the website secure and functioning. We do not use personal information for unrelated advertising without your consent.

Security and retention

We use reasonable safeguards for information submitted through the website and keep it only for as long as it is needed for ministry, administrative, legal, or security purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us to explain, correct, or delete personal information you have submitted, subject to legal and operational requirements. You may also ask us to stop sending non-essential communications.

Contact us

For privacy questions or requests, email info@prayerarmyinternational.com.
