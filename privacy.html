Privacy

Last updated: October 2026

Prayer Army International (“we”, “us”) runs this website from the Upper West Region of Ghana. This page explains, in plain words, what information we collect, why, and what you can ask us to do with it.

What we collect, and why

  • Prayer requests. The name, contact details and request you type into the prayer form. Our pastoral team reads them so that we can pray with you and follow up. They are not shown publicly.
  • “Let’s connect” form. Your name, phone or email and note, so someone can reach you as a visitor, volunteer or for counseling. Only our pastoral team and system administrators can see these.
  • Testimonies and prayer wall. What you write is shown publicly only after our team approves it. Please do not include phone numbers, addresses or other private details.
  • Sermon comments. The name and comment you post are shown publicly right away under that sermon.
  • Visit statistics. We count visits, which device type was used, which website sent you to us, and which sermons are played or shared. We do not use cookies for this and we do not store your internet address with these counts.
  • Spam protection. To stop automated abuse, a scrambled version of your internet address is kept for about a day when you submit a form. Sign-in attempts to our admin area also record the address briefly to block password guessing.

Information about your faith and prayer life is personal. We use it only for the purposes above. We do not sell it.

Facebook and Instagram (Meta) cookies

If you press Accept on the cookie notice, we load the Meta Pixel. It uses cookies to tell Meta which pages of our site you visit, so that we can understand how many people we reach and show our church updates to people who have visited. Meta receives the pages you view and basic details about your browser and device. We send event names only (for example “viewed a sermon”). We never send your name, your prayer requests, your testimonies or anything you type into our forms.

If you press Decline, or your browser sends a “Do Not Track” or Global Privacy Control signal, the Pixel is not loaded. You can change your mind at any time with “Cookie settings” at the bottom of the page. Meta’s own use of the information is explained in its privacy policy.

Who handles your information

  • Our pastoral team and website administrators, each with access only to what their role needs.
  • Our service providers: Hostinger (website hosting and the database where messages and requests are stored). They keep standard server logs.
  • Meta, only if you accept the cookie notice, as described above.

How long we keep it

Prayer requests, visitor details and testimonies are kept until our team deletes them. If you want yours removed sooner, ask us. Visit statistics are kept as simple counts.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you the information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it. You can also withdraw your cookie choice at any time. In Ghana you can raise concerns with the Data Protection Commission under the Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843).

Children

This website is meant for adults and families. If you are under 18, please ask a parent or guardian before sending us your details.

Contact us

Email prayerarmyinternational@gmail.com with any question or request about your information.

Changes

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and the date at the top.
